    Surnumerist Lexicon

    This lexicon lists the main terms built around the prefix Surn-, used within the surnumerist model.

    Financial and Institutional Terms

    • SurnBank: bank dedicated to managing surnumerist flows, ensuring transparency and traceability.
    • SurnFin: surnumerist financial system, integrating digital currencies and fiscal flows.
    • SurnFund: surnumerist investment fund, fueled by taxes and national contributions.
    • SurnTax: surnumerist fiscal mechanism, converting part of taxes into surnumerist numéraire.
    • SurnBond: surnumerist bond, used to finance green and inclusive projects.

    Social and Ecological Terms

    • SurnEco: ecological branch of Surnumerism, focused on energy and climate transition.
    • SurnSocial: social branch, ensuring equitable redistribution of surnumerist funds.
    • SurnEdu: educational program financed by Surnumerism (digital training, AI, inclusion).
    • SurnHealth: financing of healthcare infrastructure via surnumerist numéraire.

    Technological Terms

    • SurnChain: surnumerist blockchain, ensuring auditing and traceability of flows.
    • SurnAI: artificial intelligence applied to surnumerist management.
    • SurnData: global database of surnumerist flows.
    • SurnToken: digital token representing a unit of surnumerist numéraire.

    Conclusion

    The “Surn-” lexicon illustrates the modularity of the surnumerist model:
    - Institutional (SurnBank, SurnFin).
    - Fiscal and financial (SurnTax, SurnBond).
    - Social and ecological (SurnEco, SurnSocial).
    - Technological (SurnChain, SurnAI).
